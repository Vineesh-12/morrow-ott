Help & Troubleshooting

Frequently asked questions and streaming assistance

How does video seeking work without buffering?

Morrow utilizes HTTP 206 Partial Content range requests. When you drag the timeline slider, your browser requests only the exact byte range needed from Amazon CloudFront edge storage, avoiding downloading the entire media file.

What is Morrow AI Concierge?

Morrow AI is our conversational entertainment assistant. Instead of traditional keyword searches, you can ask for films by mood, plot themes, or runtimes. It cross-references IMDb ratings and validates titles against our catalogue before recommending them.

Why is a title marked unavailable?

Titles appear as unavailable if their cloud media asset is undergoing ingestion or restricted by licensing. Morrow displays an honest unavailable state rather than routing to a broken video stream.

What video formats and audio standards does Morrow support?

Morrow serves progressive MP4 streaming with H.264/AAC for universal web compatibility, and is designed for HLS adaptive multi-bitrate delivery via CloudFront CDN. Supported audio standards include Dolby Atmos and Dolby Digital 5.1.
